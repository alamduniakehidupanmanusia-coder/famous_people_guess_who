How to Play Guess Who

1. Read the first clue carefully. Each round features a secret famous personality across science, history, arts, or sports.

2. You can guess immediately for maximum points (50 pts), or click Reveal Next Clue for an easier hint (worth fewer points).

3. Answer correctly via typing or multiple choice, review interesting facts, and build your learning journal!
